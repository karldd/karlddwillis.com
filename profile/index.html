Profile

I'm Senior Manager, Applied Science on the Firefly Foundry team at Adobe, where I work at the intersection of machine learning, computational design, and creative tools. Firefly Foundry builds custom generative AI models tuned to a specific studio's style, giving creative teams tools that fit directly into their production workflows.

Before Adobe, I was Head of Model Delivery at Autodesk Research, leading the team responsible for turning foundational AI models for design into production-ready features across Autodesk's product ecosystem. My recent work has focused on deep learning for CAD, generative AI, and data-driven design tools for manufacturing. I'm especially interested in how AI can augment creativity and enable new design possibilities. Earlier in my career, I was part of the Interaction Group at Disney Research, exploring new forms of human-computer interaction.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon University. My research has been published at venues like CVPR, ICML, SIGGRAPH, UIST, and CHI, and has been featured by outlets including the BBC, NBC, Wired, and New Scientist. I’ve authored over 35 papers and filed 10+ patents.
